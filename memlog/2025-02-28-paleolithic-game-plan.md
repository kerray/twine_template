# Warmth of Tribe - Paleolithic Game Development Plan

## Game Concept
"Warmth of Tribe" is an immersive text-based adventure game set in a Paleolithic society in the Moravian Karst region of Central Europe. The game follows the life of a tribal member through different life stages, focusing on the human warmth and solidarity of tribal life while balancing the harsh realities of Paleolithic existence.

## Game Structure
The game will be structured in different life stages:
1. Childhood (ages 3-12)
2. Teenage years (ages 13-17)
3. Young adulthood (ages 18-25)
4. Parenthood (ages 26-45)
5. Elder years (ages 46+)

Each phase will offer unique experiences, challenges, and opportunities for character development.

## Core Mechanics
- **Basic Needs System**: Track hunger, thirst, fatigue, and health
- **Relationship System**: Track relationships with key tribe members
- **Skill Development**: Skills improve through use and practice
- **Seasonal Changes**: Different seasons offer different challenges and opportunities
- **Life Events**: Random and scripted events that shape the character's life

## Childhood Phase Implementation (Current Focus)

### Key Themes for Childhood
- Learning tribal customs and survival skills
- Forming initial relationships with family and tribe members
- Discovering the natural world and its dangers/resources
- Playing and developing through games with other children
- First contributions to tribal life through simple tasks

### Childhood Passages Structure

1. **Game Introduction**
   - Title screen
   - Language and gender selection
   - Introduction to the game concept

2. **Birth and Early Memories (Ages 0-3)**
   - Brief narrative introduction
   - Establishing family relationships
   - First sensory experiences

3. **Early Childhood (Ages 4-7)**
   - Learning basic skills from parents and elders
   - Playing with other children
   - First simple responsibilities
   - Exploring the immediate surroundings

4. **Middle Childhood (Ages 8-10)**
   - More complex responsibilities
   - Deeper understanding of tribal roles
   - Learning hunting, gathering, or crafting basics
   - Building relationships with peers
   - Understanding of tribal stories and beliefs

5. **Late Childhood (Ages 11-12)**
   - Preparation for coming-of-age rituals
   - More advanced skills training
   - Deeper integration into tribal activities
   - Beginning to form identity within the tribe

### Key NPCs for Childhood Phase
- **Family Members**: Parents, siblings, grandparents
- **Tribal Elders**: Wisdom-keepers, storytellers
- **Other Children**: Friends, rivals
- **Adult Mentors**: Hunters, gatherers, healers, crafters

### Planned Passages for Initial Implementation

1. **StoryTitle.tw**: "Warmth of Tribe"
2. **Start.tw**: Introduction and language/gender selection
3. **ChildhoodIntro.tw**: Birth and early memories narrative
4. **EarlyChildhood.tw**: First interactive choices as a young child
5. **FirstLesson.tw**: Learning a basic skill from a parent or elder
6. **PlayWithFriends.tw**: Interaction with other children
7. **FirstHunt.tw**: Observing or participating in a small hunt
8. **GatheringExpedition.tw**: Joining adults to gather resources
9. **TribalCelebration.tw**: Experiencing a tribal festival or ceremony
10. **ChildhoodCrisis.tw**: Facing a challenge (injury, lost, etc.)
11. **ChildhoodTransition.tw**: Preparation for teenage years

### Variables to Track
- Basic needs (hunger, thirst, fatigue, health)
- Relationships with key NPCs
- Skills development (tracking, crafting, gathering, etc.)
- Knowledge of tribal lore and customs
- Character traits developing based on choices
