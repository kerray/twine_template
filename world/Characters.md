# Warmth of Tribe - Character Profiles

## Player Character

The player character is a member of the tribe born at the beginning of the game. Their development, personality, and life path are shaped by player choices throughout the game.

### Initial Attributes
- **Name**: Selected or generated at game start
- **Gender**: Selected at game start (male/female/other)
- **Family Position**: Youngest child in a family with 1-2 older siblings
- **Starting Age**: 3 years old (earliest memories)
- **Special Characteristic**: One unique trait determined at birth (keen eyes, strong constitution, curious mind, etc.)

### Development Paths
The player character can develop along different paths based on player choices:
- **Hunter/Tracker**: Focus on hunting, tracking, and survival skills
- **Gatherer/Healer**: Focus on plant knowledge, healing, and nurturing
- **Crafter/Toolmaker**: Focus on creating and improving tools and items
- **Spiritual/Social**: Focus on tribal lore, rituals, and interpersonal connections

## Family Members

### Mother (Ama)
- **Age**: 25-30
- **Role**: Primary caregiver, gatherer, and craftswoman
- **Personality**: Nurturing, practical, and resilient
- **Skills**: Expert in plant identification, food preparation, and hide working
- **Relationship Dynamics**: Teaches practical skills, provides emotional support, and encourages independence
- **Character Arc**: Gradually shifts from protector to mentor as player grows

### Father (Taro)
- **Age**: 30-35
- **Role**: Hunter, provider, and toolmaker
- **Personality**: Patient, observant, and reserved
- **Skills**: Skilled hunter, tracker, and flint knapper
- **Relationship Dynamics**: Teaches hunting and tool-making, expects growing competence
- **Character Arc**: Tests player's abilities with increasingly challenging tasks

### Older Sibling (Kima)
- **Age**: 8-12
- **Gender**: Opposite of player character (default)
- **Personality**: Competitive, protective, and adventurous
- **Skills**: Already developing hunting or gathering skills
- **Relationship Dynamics**: Alternates between rival and protector, introduces player to peer activities
- **Character Arc**: Undergoes coming-of-age rituals during player's childhood

### Grandmother (Nima)
- **Age**: 50-60
- **Role**: Elder, storyteller, and knowledge keeper
- **Personality**: Wise, patient, and occasionally stern
- **Skills**: Tribal history, medicinal knowledge, and ritual practices
- **Relationship Dynamics**: Shares wisdom through stories, teaches tribal values
- **Character Arc**: Prepares player for understanding deeper tribal knowledge

### Grandfather (Pavo)
- **Age**: 55-65
- **Role**: Elder hunter and tribal council member
- **Personality**: Stoic, respected, with unexpected humor
- **Skills**: Strategic hunting, weather prediction, and leadership
- **Relationship Dynamics**: Offers rare but valuable praise, tests character
- **Character Arc**: Gradually reveals more of the tribe's history and challenges

## Tribal Members

### Tribal Leader (Mako)
- **Age**: 40-50
- **Gender**: Male
- **Role**: Chief decision-maker and hunt leader
- **Personality**: Authoritative, fair, and protective of the tribe
- **Skills**: Leadership, conflict resolution, and hunting strategy
- **Relationship Dynamics**: Initially distant but notices player's development
- **Character Arc**: Tests player's growing contributions to tribal welfare

### Shaman/Healer (Sora)
- **Age**: 35-45
- **Gender**: Female
- **Role**: Spiritual leader and medicine person
- **Personality**: Perceptive, enigmatic, and compassionate
- **Skills**: Healing, ritual knowledge, and plant medicine
- **Relationship Dynamics**: Recognizes player's potential, offers spiritual guidance
- **Character Arc**: May select player for special knowledge if appropriate choices are made

### Master Hunter (Kuno)
- **Age**: 30-40
- **Gender**: Male
- **Role**: Most skilled hunter and tracking teacher
- **Personality**: Demanding, respected, and intense
- **Skills**: Expert tracking, weapon use, and animal knowledge
- **Relationship Dynamics**: Initially dismissive of children, but can become a mentor
- **Character Arc**: Gradually tests player's hunting aptitude if that path is chosen

### Master Gatherer (Lina)
- **Age**: 30-40
- **Gender**: Female
- **Role**: Expert on plants, fungi, and natural resources
- **Personality**: Observant, methodical, and nurturing
- **Skills**: Plant identification, food preservation, and medicine making
- **Relationship Dynamics**: Willing teacher to interested children
- **Character Arc**: Can become a mentor if player shows interest in gathering/healing

### Toolmaker (Daro)
- **Age**: 35-45
- **Gender**: Male
- **Role**: Creator of the tribe's finest tools and weapons
- **Personality**: Detail-oriented, patient, and proud of his work
- **Skills**: Flint knapping, bone carving, and tool design
- **Relationship Dynamics**: Appreciates careful, attentive children
- **Character Arc**: May take player as apprentice if crafting path is chosen

## Child Characters

### Best Friend (Tama)
- **Age**: Same as player
- **Gender**: Player's choice or random
- **Personality**: Loyal, adventurous, and supportive
- **Skills**: Complementary to player's developing interests
- **Relationship Dynamics**: Constant companion, shares discoveries and troubles
- **Character Arc**: Relationship evolves based on player choices and shared experiences

### Rival Child (Runo)
- **Age**: 1-2 years older than player
- **Gender**: Random
- **Personality**: Competitive, boastful, but not malicious
- **Skills**: Slightly more advanced due to age advantage
- **Relationship Dynamics**: Creates challenges, occasional conflicts
- **Character Arc**: Can become friend or continued rival based on player choices

### Younger Tribe Child (Miki)
- **Age**: 2-3 years younger than player
- **Gender**: Random
- **Personality**: Curious, admiring of older children
- **Skills**: Still learning basic tasks
- **Relationship Dynamics**: Looks up to player, provides opportunity to teach/protect
- **Character Arc**: Player's treatment affects their development and confidence

### Outsider Child (Naro)
- **Age**: Same as player
- **Gender**: Random
- **Personality**: Quiet, observant, slightly different customs
- **Skills**: Unique knowledge from different tribal background
- **Relationship Dynamics**: Potential friend who offers different perspectives
- **Character Arc**: Integration into tribe affected by player's acceptance or rejection

## Character Interaction System

### Relationship Values
- Each NPC has a relationship value (0-100) with the player
- Values change based on interactions, gifts, and shared activities
- Different thresholds unlock new interactions and dialogue options
- Relationship values affect how NPCs respond in crisis situations

### Interaction Types
- **Teaching**: NPCs with high skills can teach the player
- **Playing**: Child NPCs engage in games that build skills
- **Gifting**: Giving found items or crafted objects improves relationships
- **Helping**: Assisting NPCs with tasks improves relationships and skills
- **Observing**: Watching skilled NPCs provides learning opportunities
- **Storytelling**: Elders share knowledge through stories at appropriate times

### Character Memory
- NPCs remember significant interactions with the player
- Past choices affect future dialogue options and opportunities
- Repeated patterns of behavior establish player's reputation
- Key life events are remembered and referenced by close NPCs

## Childhood Phase NPC Encounters

### Early Childhood (Ages 3-7)
- **Primary Interactions**: Parents, siblings, grandmother, best friend
- **Occasional Interactions**: Tribal leader, other children
- **Rare Interactions**: Shaman, master hunter/gatherer

### Middle Childhood (Ages 8-10)
- **Primary Interactions**: Expanded to include more tribal members
- **New Relationships**: Begin apprenticeship with skilled adults
- **Changing Dynamics**: More independence from parents, stronger peer relationships

### Late Childhood (Ages 11-12)
- **Primary Interactions**: Mentors, friends, rivals
- **Preparation Relationships**: Elders preparing child for coming-of-age
- **New Dynamics**: Beginning to be seen as a contributing tribe member

## Implementation Notes

### Character Development
- Each NPC should have consistent speech patterns and concerns
- NPCs should react appropriately to player's developing skills and traits
- Family members should show the most complex relationship evolution
- NPCs should have their own goals and activities independent of player

### Dialogue System
- Different relationship levels unlock different dialogue options
- NPCs' knowledge should be consistent with their skills and experiences
- Childhood interactions should be age-appropriate and evolve as player ages
- Key NPCs should occasionally initiate interactions based on recent events

### Memory System
- Track significant interactions with each NPC
- Record player's successes and failures witnessed by NPCs
- Note gifts given and received
- Remember help provided or refused
